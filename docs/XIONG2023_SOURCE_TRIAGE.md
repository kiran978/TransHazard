# Xiong et al. (2023): four same-study transformation hypotheses — NOT APPROVED

Source: Xiong Y et al., *Environmental Science & Technology* 57 (2023) 15213–15222, DOI [10.1021/acs.est.3c03242](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.3c03242). Table 2 reports 48-hour `Daphnia magna` LC50 values based on *experimentally measured aqueous test concentrations*. Table 2 dispersions are **standard deviations**, not confidence intervals. The public article is under CC BY 4.0.

The paper measured LC50s for four parent/chemical-counterpart pairs. We recorded original numeric values in `data/xiong2023_table2_UNAPPROVED.csv`. The provided mass-based point-estimate ratio is a screening value only; **no molar label** is issued until exact isomers, structures and molecular weights are verified.

**Crucial transformation-direction caution**: In the study, demethylation of diazepam, methylparaben, and naproxen was observed *within Daphnia after exposure*. That alone does not establish the same transformation in external environmental media. The investigators detected negligible formation of methylated counterparts in Daphnia; therefore the acetaminophen -> M-acetaminophen route is **not experimentally demonstrated in that study**. All four routes remain on **HOLD** for TransHazard's environmental-transformation objective.

All four observations come from **one additional publication**, not four independent experimental studies. The 48-hour study endpoint should not be combined with the 24-hour malathion cohort or 7-day DDT/PFAS cohorts. The compound-level values represent separate exposed groups; do not pretend the point-estimate ratio has paired replicate uncertainty.

Pending gates: exact structures/CAS and transformation route in environment; dose-response uncertainties beyond summary SD; source assay preparation/organism stage comparability; independent reviewer decision. These records must **not** enter training while on HOLD.
